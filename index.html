FinWise

Intelligent Personal Finance & Wealth Management Platform

FinWise started with a simple thought: managing money should be easier to understand, not just easier to track. I explored the idea of a personal finance platform that brings budgeting, income and expense tracking, savings goals, and investment monitoring into one place. The aim is to give users a clearer picture of where their money goes, how their savings are progressing, and how their investments are performing.
